Additional Requirements — Dentist Booking

1. The system shall allow a registered user to complete a pre-appointment questionnaire associated with their booking, and allow only the booking owner and the admin to access the responses. (0.5)

2. The system shall allow the admin to mark booked users as arrived, maintain their arrival order, and update their queue status to waiting, called, or completed. (0.5)

3. The system shall allow a registered user to join or leave a waiting list for a selected dentist and date, and allow the admin to view waiting users in registration order when a booking is cancelled. (1)

4. The system shall generate a unique QR code containing the booking reference when a registered user views their booking. (1)

Total: 3 points (subject to instructor assessment)

Ownership:
- A: requirements 1 and 4
- B: requirements 2 and 3
